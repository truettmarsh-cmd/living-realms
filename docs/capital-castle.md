# Capital Castle blueprint

The original Living Realms blueprint is inspired by the courtyard arrangement in [Bodiam Castle's ground plan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bodiam_Castle_ground_plan.jpg), derived from *Archaeological Journal* (1905), volume 62, page 179, Council of the Royal Archaeological Institute. Wikimedia identifies the reference plan as public domain. No downloaded Minecraft schematic or third-party build is bundled.

The Minecraft design uses a 41 × 41 perimeter, four accessible corner towers, battlement walks, a great hall, four guard beds, an open gatehouse and a rear postern. It is an adaptation, not a scale reconstruction. The central 25 × 25 area contains no blueprint entries—not even air—so the charter and existing town hall remain intact. Biome wood accents match the settlement.

A settlement reaching Capital rank with Royal Charter automatically plans one castle at its exact center. Existing construction, block entities, uneven ground and unloaded chunks can postpone the project; the Capital overview and town log explain why. Autonomous settlement placement reserves the future perimeter. Existing towns may need the perimeter cleared first. Ordinary town builders place the castle block by block using stockpile materials (independent AI towns use their existing paid treasury construction). Shortages pause work normally. The castle adds barracks capacity and guard beds through existing building rules.

Each kingdom has one designated capital. Only that city may reach Capital rank and commission this project; a conquered or replaced capital keeps its existing structures but returns to City rank. The castle can also be selected in the building catalog, but must be centered correctly and satisfy the same checks.
